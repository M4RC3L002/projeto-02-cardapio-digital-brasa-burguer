# Design
Mobile-first food menu. Warm cream #faf7f2, white surfaces, burnt orange #b94720, deep brown #29241e, sage #406650. Manrope Variable. Generous product photography, 20–28px panels, 16px product photo corners, 48px controls. Sticky categories and bottom shopping bag; product options and checkout in native dialog sheets. On desktop, 1040px centered content, four-column grid and compact sticky header; on mobile, two-column products, large images and a single-column featured product. Fictional demonstration notice remains visible. No fabricated reviews, discounts or operating status.
