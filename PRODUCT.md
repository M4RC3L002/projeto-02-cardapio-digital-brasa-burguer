# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Isolated project, independent of portfolio.
## Purpose
Mobile-first digital hamburger menu with product customization, shopping bag and order handoff to WhatsApp. User confirmed hamburger restaurant and WhatsApp ordering.
## Constraints
Original design inspired by two supplied mobile menu images. Rounded, modern, photographic, comfortable touch controls. No real restaurant name, contact, address, hours or menu supplied. Brasa Burger is an explicitly fictional demonstration. Prices and photos are illustrative. WhatsApp disabled until business phone configured; copying order is available. No online payments, backend, account or claim of completed order.
## Accessibility
Native dialogs, keyboard/focus handling, Portuguese labels, no zoom restriction, safe-area padding, 16px inputs, reduced motion, comfortable touch controls. Desktop emulation does not establish real-device correctness.
